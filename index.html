What changes for your energy bill in 2027

  • Saldering ends on 1 January 2027. A home that takes 2,200 kWh from the grid and feeds back 2,700 kWh pays about €284 more a year, after lower feed-in costs.
  • Exported solar power earns at least 50% of the supply price without tax until 2030 (ACM). Most suppliers also charge feed-in costs, but much less than in 2026: about €0.045 to €0.07 per kWh. How that works.
  • Heat pumps and EV chargers usually fit on a 3x25A connection if the charger has a load balancer. Check your home.
