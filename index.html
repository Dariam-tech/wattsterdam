Why it changes

One unit of your solar power is worth…

Until the end of 2026, solar power you send to the grid cancels out power you take from it later, unit for unit. From 1 January 2027 that stops. Power you use yourself is still worth a lot. Power you send back is worth very little.

Questions

Straight answers.

Want the detail behind the numbers? Open "Use my own prices" in the calculator.

Is this my full energy bill for 2027? +

No. It's only the change caused by the new rules for solar power, and by lower fees for power you send back. Grid costs, your supplier's monthly fee, gas and other price changes are not included.

How accurate is the quick estimate? +

It's a typical home with your number of panels and people: each panel makes about 350 units a year, and about 30% of solar power is used directly at home. Your roof and habits can differ a lot. For your own number, choose "Use my bill" and enter two figures from your yearly bill.

What if my contract has no fee for power I send back? +

Open "Use my own prices" and set today's fee to 0. Then the lower 2027 fee no longer softens the increase, so your result goes up.

Does a home battery always pay off? +

No. For most homes the saving on the bill is too small to earn back €3,300 to €7,000 within the battery's life. It can make sense with high fees for power you send back, a lot of evening use, or a contract with hourly prices.

Do you store or share what I enter? +

No. Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. "Copy my result" only copies a summary to your clipboard.

How we calculate this +

Change per year = the smaller of what you take from and send to the grid × (your price per unit − the payment for power you send back) + everything you send back × (2027 fee − today's fee). The payment defaults to the legal minimum until 2030: half of your price without energy tax (€0.0880 per unit in 2027) and VAT (ACM). Typical fees for power you send back: about €0.11 per unit today and €0.055 in 2027 (published 2027 contracts mostly charge €0.045 to €0.07). Quick estimate: 350 units per panel per year, 30% used directly (at most half your use), and yearly use by household size from Nibud/CBS (1,640 to 3,980 units). Sources: ACM ConsuWijzer, Belastingplan 2027, Nibud/CBS, ANWB, Energievergelijk, Zonnesaldo.
