What changes for your energy bill in 2027

  • Saldering ends on 1 January 2027. A typical home with 10 solar panels (about 4 kWp) and 3,300 kWh of yearly use pays about €565 more a year.
  • Exported solar power earns at least 50% of the bare supply rate until 2030, but many fixed contracts subtract a feed-in fee.
  • Heat pumps and EV chargers usually fit on a 3x25A connection if the charger has a load balancer. Check your home.
