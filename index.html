What changes for your energy bill in 2027

  • Saldering ends on 1 January 2027. A typical home with 10 solar panels and 3,300 kWh of yearly use pays about €426 more a year.
  • Exported solar power earns at least 50% of the supply price without tax until 2030 (ACM), but most suppliers also charge feed-in costs.
  • Heat pumps and EV chargers usually fit on a 3x25A connection if the charger has a load balancer. Check your home.
